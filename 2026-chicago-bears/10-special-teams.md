## Commentary
The bears special teams unit isn't really anything out of the world. They are average at best. But honestly, that's all you can ask for. The bears offense is already loaded up with so many weapons where all that is needed from the special teams unit is typically a regular routine kick or play. All the bears can ask for from this position group is consistency. The bears kicker is Cairo Santos. Throughout the last two seasons, he has completed around 84% of his field goals. Although he doesn't have a strong leg for distance, he has an accurate one. The punter on the other hand is a player who is typically slept on. As a punter, you want to punt the ball as close as you can to the endzone without getting a touchback. Tory has a gift which allows him to curve his punts in a sense which makes it difficult to recieve for the other team. This eventually leads to more fumbles and opportunities for the bears to get the ball back. Although, the special teams unit on the bears might not be recognized as one of the best, they do the little things in the game amazingly that in the end play a huge part in helping the bears win games.
