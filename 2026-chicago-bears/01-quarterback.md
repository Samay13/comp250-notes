# Note 1

## Title
Caleb Williams and MVP?

## Quotation
> "When is the last time you can remember the Bears' quarterback room being a top-three position group on the team? That's the Caleb Williams effect."

## Citation & Point Locator
* **Source Citation:** Sugrue, Brendan. "Ranking 2026 Chicago Bears position groups from best to worst." *Bears Wire*, USA Today, July 16, 2026.
* **Point Locator:** Section 3

## Commentary
This quote talks about the future of the Chicago Bears and their QB room. Caleb Williams was drafted 1st overall in the 2024 NFL draft. Although his first year was extremely underwhelming with the failing head coach, a horrible offensive line, and miscommunication within the team that had people wondering if Caleb will be a bust. But throughout the 2025 offseason, the bears made some incredible moves that eventually built up many holes on the team. One of the biggest moves that the bears made was the coaching switch from Matt Eberflus to Ben Johnson. Ben is the perfect fit for Caleb in which the 2025/26 season displays. Caleb and the bears started the season pretty mellow with a couple losses. This was somewhat expected as there were still many new pieces that were added to the squad. As the year went on though, Caleb and the bears exceeded expectations and made a name for themselves. They eventually finished 11-6 with the Bears picking up from their slow start. Caleb ended the season with 3,942 passing yards, which is a team record, and created a name for himself by having these insane 4th quarter comebacks that eventually resulted in him gaining the nickname, "Iceman". Since that season, Caleb has only seemed to move upwards from there. There are many high hopes from him as he leads the Chicago Bears through the 2026/27 NFL season.
