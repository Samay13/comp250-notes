## Commentary
In 2024, the bears acquired D'andre Swift and then in 2025, the bears drafted Kyle Monangai. This duo sparked near the middle of the season of 2025. In the beginning, it didn't seem like Swift couldn't get his rhythm correct. The weak offensive line didn't help much either. It was until the 2025 season where the bears invested in a stronger O-line where Swift wa finally able to find his rhythm. Monangai emerged as a rookie especially during the Eagles game in 2025 where Swift and Monangai combined for 100 rushing yards each. Ever since that game, they have consistently been able run the ball positvely for the Bears ever since. There are extremely high hopes for the Chicago RB's this year as they have only been able to grow more comfortable in their roles.
