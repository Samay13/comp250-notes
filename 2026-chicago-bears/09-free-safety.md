## Title
The Deep Third: Free Safety Range

## Quotation
> "The free safety operates as the ultimate centerfielder, tracking the quarterback's eyes to cover vast expanses of grass and snuff out deep scoring threats."

## Citation & Point Locator
* **Source Citation:** Chicago Bears Roster Grades. YouTube video, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tZaoHdBiKG4, retrieved September 17, 2026.
* **Point Locator:** Timestamp 27:15

## Commentary
Operating as the last line of defense in the secondary, a high-IQ free safety provides a safety net that allows cornerbacks to play with aggressive leverage underneath. Exceptional range and instinctual route recognition enable a free safety to range sideline-to-sideline, turning potential deep completions into contested balls or interceptions. This positional presence heavily influences how opposing quarterbacks attack the field, often forcing them to check down underneath rather than testing the deep third. Effective centerfield play requires a rare blend of textbook angles, explosive closing speed, and fearless open-field tackling when runners break through the front seven.
