## Commentary
The bears safety room has been the biggest aspect on the team in which has changed since last season. During the 2025 season, the bears thrived with Jaquon Brisker as the free safety and Kevin Byard III as the strong safety. They both filled there roles perfectly with Kevin Byard even leading the NFL with the most interceptions. Throughout the offseason, the bears had to unfortunately let go Jaquon Brisker to the Steelers and Kevin Byard to the Patriots. Although the Bears lost two of their biggest players on the defense, they have made some incredible moves in the offseason to replace them. Replacing strong safety, Kevin Byard, is Coby Bryant in which they have gotten from the Seattle Seahawks. Replacing free safety, Jaquon Brisker, is Dillon Thieneman in which the bears have drafted in the 2026 NFL draft. This new duo has shown flashes off greatness throughout their past seasons as well as in the offseason. We have yet to see them suit up together on the field as around a month before the season starts, Coby Bryant had come down with an injury that will unfortunately sideline him for at least a couple weeks. Until that time comes, Elijah Hicks and Cam Lewis will have to fill in for him.
