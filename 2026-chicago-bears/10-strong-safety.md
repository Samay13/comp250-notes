# Title

# Summary

An analysis of box safety play, run support physicality, and intermediate coverage for the 2026 season.

# Evidence

The Bears rely on Jaquan Brisker at strong safety to bring physical run support, blitz capability, and tight coverage against tight ends and slot receivers in the box.
