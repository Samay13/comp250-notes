## Title
Box Safety Impact and Physical Presence

## Quotation
> "The strong safety acts as a versatile eraser in the box, delivering physical support against the run while matching up against athletic pass-catching tight ends."

## Commentary
The strong safety bridges the stylistic gap between defensive backs and linebackers, serving as an aggressive physical presence near the line of scrimmage while retaining enough speed to handle coverage assignments. Their ability to step down into the box effectively turns an eight-man front into a brick wall against inside zone runs and off-tackle counters. In passing situations, they provide the necessary size to neutralize mismatch tight ends who otherwise exploit smaller linebackers or slot defenders over the middle. This unique blend of run-stopping grit and coverage adaptability gives defensive coordinators immense tactical flexibility to disguise coverages and confuse opposing quarterbacks.
