# Note 6

## Title
Pass Rush and Defensive Front Control

## Quotation
> "A disruptive defensive line collapses the pocket from the inside out, choking off opponent rushing lanes while applying relentless pressure on the opposing quarterback."

## Citation & Point Locator
* **Source Citation:** Chicago Bears Roster Grades. YouTube video, https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tZaoHdBiKG4, retrieved September 17, 2026.
* **Point Locator:** Timestamp 16:30

## Commentary
Defensive line disruption is the single most effective catalyst for a dominant defense, because creating chaos in the offensive backfield short-circuits opposing play-calling before it can develop. When interior linemen can generate pressure without requiring extra blitzers, defensive coordinators gain the luxury of dropping seven or eight players into coverage, heavily restricting passing options. Furthermore, disciplined gap control against the run eliminates easy gains on early downs, forcing offenses into predictable, long-yardage passing situations where the pass rush can pin their ears back. The cumulative physical fatigue inflicted by a deep, rotational defensive front often breaks an opponent's resolve late in games.
