Defensive Line Struggles and Looks for Improvement

## Quotation
> "They're putting a lot of faith in multiple players to bounce back, but chances are the defensive line will remain a below-average unit before the Bears can overhaul it in 2027."

## Citation & Point Locator
* **Source Citation:** Sugrue, Brendan. "Ranking 2026 Chicago Bears position groups from best to worst." *Bears Wire*, USA Today, July 16, 2026.

## Commentary
The bears defensive line wasn't the best last year. Although the bears haven't made too many changes to the D-Line this last year. There is hope for improvement. The bears have picked up Grady Jarrett ad Dayo Odeyingbo from free agency and as they are finally going to be back from injury, there are flashes of hope that they can spark and break out this year to have amazing seasons.
