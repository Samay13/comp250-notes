## Commentary
The bears wide reciever room has been where the most questions have been for the 2026 season. Rome Odunze was drafted the same year as Caleb Williams in 2024 and Luther Burden who was drafted in 2025. Two extremely young players with still much to show. Rome had a similar path as Caleb with a not-so-great team in 2024 and then he was dealing with some injuries throughout 2025 which limited him. Luther took most of the load throughout 2025 and emerged as one of the best RAC wide recievers in the league. These two recievers will be playing along each other to fit the WR1 and WR2 roles. Both of them have extremely different playstyles in which can be implemented into the Ben Johnson office. The WR3 role also have many questions. The bears have two existing NFL players who are, Kalif Raymond and Jahdae Walker. Kalif was picked up by the bears this last offseason and Jahdae has been on the bears since 2025. They both will fit in the WR3 roles nicely as both of them will fit different playstyles within the WR3 role in the Ben Johnson office. The bears have also drafted Zavion Thomas from LSU who has flashed insane sparks of greatness within the preseason games for the bears. With an incredibly quick speed, he will be able to blow by defenders.
